## Pattern (this batch)

Steal *construction* from geometric tree marks (chevron / herringbone fill, leaf or oval silhouette, even stripe weight, rounded joins). Do **not** copy those logos, mint, or gold.

The tree is a **metaphor for a path of knowledge**, printed as a quiet watermark. Low contrast. Small. Right side of the card, not a second headline.

**Not**: PCB traces, square pads, vias, 90° turns, dense geek circuitry, pattern shouting over the title.

## Mark

A reduced chevron-tree in ink, one minium stripe or node if needed. Rounded forks. Reads at 16px. Same family as the whisper pattern, not a different object.

## Killed

Circuit-rune PCB language. Batch 2 marks, catalogs, cards, heroes that used it. A/B/C. D’s old botanical tile mark.
